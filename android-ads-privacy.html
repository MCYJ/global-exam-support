Information stored on your device

Study progress, answer history, bookmarks, study preferences, Lock Quiz preferences, and notification settings are stored locally on your device. No RushLabs account is required for the app.

Information we do not collect

The app does not send your study answers, bookmarks, notification content, contacts, precise location, photos, or personal files to a RushLabs server.

Google AdMob and privacy choices

We use Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform in advertising-enabled editions. Google Mobile Ads may process and share your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), interactions with the app or ads, performance diagnostics, and device or account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID. Google uses these data for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention.

Where applicable, the app asks for your choices before requesting ads. If Google requires a privacy controls entry, use the privacy options in the app’s settings to review or change your choices. Your Android settings also let you reset or delete the advertising ID. Read how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Android 광고 및 개인정보 선택

이 안내는 위 목록에 있는 앱의 광고 지원 Android 버전에 적용됩니다. 학습 기록은 기기에 저장됩니다. Google AdMob은 광고 게재, 성과 측정, 부정 사용 방지를 위해 IP 주소(대략적인 위치를 추정할 수 있음), 앱·광고 이용 내역, 성능 진단 정보와 광고 ID 등 기기 또는 계정 식별자를 처리하거나 공유할 수 있습니다.

해당 지역에서는 광고를 요청하기 전에 선택을 받습니다. 앱 설정에 개인정보 보호 옵션이 표시되면 선택을 변경할 수 있습니다. Android 설정에서 광고 ID를 재설정하거나 삭제할 수 있습니다. 광고 영구 제거 결제와 복원은 Google Play에서 처리합니다. 구매 확인 정보는 기기에 저장되며 RushLabs 서버로 전송되지 않습니다.

Purchases

Google Play processes the optional one-time ad removal purchase and restores it for the purchasing Play account. The app verifies purchase information and can retain it locally to remember the ad removal entitlement. RushLabs does not receive payment-card details or host your purchase receipt.

If you have an existing Android subscription, Google Play also manages its billing and restoration. Manage it in your Play account.

Notifications and Lock Quiz

Lock Quiz notifications are scheduled locally. Question text is hidden by default and appears on the lock screen only after explicit opt-in. You can disable notifications or hide question text at any time in the app or operating-system settings.

Data deletion and retention

You can remove local study data by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app. Store purchase history is retained by Google or Apple under their own policies.

Device or operating-system backup settings may retain a copy of local app data. If your edition offers an export or backup, you choose where to save or share that file. Delete those copies separately when you no longer need them. RushLabs does not host your study backup.

Children

The app is a professional exam-preparation tool and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
