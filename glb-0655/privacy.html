RUSHLABS · GLB-0655

Privacy policy

Latvia Security Guard Prep · app.mcyj.examprep.glb0655
Updated September 15, 2026

This policy describes the current GLB-0655 configuration. This independent professional study app does not require a RushLabs account.

Study information stays on your device

Answers, study progress, history, bookmarks, notes, language preferences and daily study limits are stored locally. RushLabs does not receive these records through an app server. The current configuration has no advertising or behavioral analytics SDK.

Files, sharing and external links

Imports use the system file picker. Exports create a temporary JSON backup on your device and open the system share sheet. If you explicitly share an exported file, the recipient or app you select receives that file under its own privacy practices. Opening official source or support links uses an external browser and requires internet access; the destination handles the visit under its own policy.

Purchases and unavailable features

Subscription purchases and Lock Quiz notifications are unavailable in the current configuration. Existing study records remain local. If subscription purchases are enabled in a later configuration, Google Play or Apple processes purchases and restores, and the app uses the entitlement result. RushLabs does not receive your payment-card details. The policy will be updated before changes to data handling or notification availability are released.

Deletion and retention

You can remove app-local records by clearing app data or uninstalling the app. Exported or shared copies must be removed separately from their selected storage or recipients. Google Play and Apple retain store records under their own policies.

Children and contact

The app is a professional examination study tool and is not directed to children. It has no social feed, messaging or shared user-content service.

For privacy questions, use RushLabs support. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or other unnecessary sensitive information.

The app is independently produced and is not affiliated with the Latvian State Police or an examination authority.
