IRM L5 Management

Privacy policy

App reference: glb-1223
Updated November 3, 2026

This policy covers the iOS edition of IRM L5 Management, displayed inside the app as Enterprise Risk Study. It is a paid independent study app for enterprise risk management. No RushLabs account is required.

Information on your device

The app stores answer history, bookmarks, study progress, study activity and optional local quiz reminder settings in local app storage on your device. Study content is included in the app; normal study does not require a RushLabs server connection.

Information we do not collect

RushLabs does not collect or receive your answers, bookmarks, study records or notification content through this app. The app provides no study-data upload service and does not upload contacts, precise location, photos or personal files to RushLabs. The app does not include advertising or behavioral analytics.

This policy page contains no advertising, analytics scripts or forms. GitHub Pages hosts this website and may process ordinary connection information under GitHub’s privacy statement. Opening an official-source, terms or support link is subject to that destination’s privacy policy.

Purchases

Apple handles the app purchase and download through your store account. One paid download provides the included study content without an additional subscription or in-app purchase. RushLabs does not receive your payment-card details through the app. Store payment information and purchase history are handled by the store.

Optional local notifications

Quiz reminders are off by default. They are scheduled on your device after you enable them and grant the required device permission. Question text is hidden by default and appears in notifications only after your explicit opt-in. The app uses your device’s time-zone setting to schedule reminders locally. You can disable reminders, hide question text and manage notification permission in device settings.

Retention, backups and app-data deletion

Saved records remain in local app storage until removed. To remove all local study information, use your device’s app-data deletion controls or uninstall the app and its data. Offloading an app may retain its data.

Operating-system backups may retain copies according to your device’s backup and restore settings. Apple retains store purchase history separately.

Younger learners

No account or data-upload service is provided, including for younger learners. A parent or guardian can manage store purchases and device notification and backup settings.
