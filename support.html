App support

Help for RushLabs Exam Prep.

App reference: RushLabs Exam Prep

Restore a subscription

Open Premium or Subscription Management in the app and choose Restore purchase. Use the same Google Play or Apple account that made the original purchase.

Reset study progress

Study activity is local to your device. Clear the app’s storage in system settings or reinstall the app to remove local progress, bookmarks, and Lock Quiz preferences.

Lock Quiz is not appearing

Confirm that notifications are allowed, then enable Lock Quiz in the app. Question text on the lock screen is a separate privacy setting and remains off until you opt in.

Report a problem

Email RushLabs support and include the app reference shown above, device model, operating-system version, app version, and steps to reproduce. We normally acknowledge support requests within two business days. Do not include passwords, payment-card details, receipts, purchase tokens, or private documents.
